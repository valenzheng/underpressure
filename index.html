How to Play

Objective

Make clinical decisions to stabilize your patient and prevent deterioration.

Controls

  • Click on the patient to begin the assessment.
  • Read each scenario carefully before deciding.
  • Select the intervention you believe is most appropriate.

Monitor the Patient

  • Watch heart rate and blood pressure closely.
  • Vital signs change based on your decisions.
  • Deteriorating vitals indicate increasing danger.

Important

  • Incorrect decisions can worsen the patient's condition.
  • Multiple mistakes may lead to critical outcomes.
  • Not every mistake is immediately fatal.
  • Use clinical judgment and prioritize patient safety.

Good luck.
